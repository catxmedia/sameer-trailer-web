# SAMEER TRAILER - MASTER CATALOGUE TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

## 1. SIDE WALL TRAILER (32ft / 36ft / 40ft)
- **Chassis:** Fabricated Main Girder from HSFQ-450 / ST-52 high-tensile steel. Cross members positioned at optimal load stress points.
- **Axle:** Tandem or Tridem (14T to 18T capacity per axle) York, TATA, or Kross equivalent.
- **King Pin:** 2-inch or 3.5-inch IS:6763 Part I compliant forged alloy king pin.
- **Brakes:** Dual-line air brake system with automatic slack adjuster, T24/30 spring brake chambers on all wheels, QRV/LQRV valves.
- **Suspension:** 18-ton capacity tandem over-slung leaf spring suspension with equalizing brackets / Tridem pneumatic air suspension.
- **Landing Leg:** Two-speed gear operated mechanical landing leg (Jost / York equivalent).
- **Painting:** Shot blasting to Sa 2.5 finish + ASTM B117 500-hr salt spray tested Polyurethane (PU) paint.

## 2. TIP TRAILER (28 Cu.M to 40 Cu.M)
- **Chassis:** Heavy duty ladder frame construction with high-tensile main girders.
- **Hydraulic Kit:** Hyva / Wipro / Penta front-end multi-stage telescopic cylinder (FC 191-5-6010 / 169-4 stage), 170 BAR working pressure, tipping angle 48°-52°.
- **Axle:** Tridem 16T/18T heavy duty axles.
- **Body Material:** High wear-resistant 5mm/6mm floor, 4mm/5mm sidewalls (Hardox / ST-52).

## 3. ROCK BODY TIPPER & U-SHAPE MINING TIPPER (20 Cu.M & 22.5/24 Cu.M)
- **Design:** Curved U-shape floor structure for instant impact distribution and zero sticky material retention.
- **Floor & Walls:** 8mm floor / 6mm walls high-wear resistant mining steel.
- **Tipping Angle:** 52° rapid dump cycle.

## 4. BULKER TRAILER & RIGID BULKER (35 / 40 / 45 Cu.M & 20-30 Cu.M)
- **Pneumatics:** 1.65 BAR working pressure, hydrostatic tested at 2.5 BAR.
- **Discharge Rate:** 1.2 to 1.5 tons per minute, aeration fabric pads with dual Class 3 safety relief valves.

## 5. TANKERS (Fuel Bowser, Edible Oil, Petrol Tanker)
- **Capacity:** 3KL, 4KL, 6KL Diesel Bowsers & 24,000L to 35,000L Road Tankers.
- **Compliance:** PESO & API bottom loading compliant, food-grade SS-304/316L for edible oil/milk.
