MANUFACTURING & STEEL TECH

Automated shot-blasting, SAW seam welding, and HSFQ-450 certified high-tensile steel processing.

🛡️

1. SAIL & Jindal HSFQ-450 Steel

Our trailers are constructed using certified High Strength Quenched & Tempered (HSFQ-450) steel plates. This allows us to reduce dead weight by up to 1,000 kg while providing superior yield strength under severe road overload conditions.

🔬

2. Sa 2.5 Automated Shot-Blasting

Before primer application, every chassis structure passes through an automated shot-blasting chamber. This cleans the steel surface to Near-White Metal (ISO 8501-1 Sa 2.5 Standard), ensuring maximum paint adhesion and zero rust formation.

🎨

3. 500-Hr Salt Spray Tested PU Finish

Coated with 2-pack Polyurethane (PU) paint applied inside climate-controlled spray booths. Tested to withstand 500 hours of continuous salt-spray corrosion, ensuring long-term color gloss and steel protection against harsh weather.

⚙️

4. York & TATA 18T Axles & ABS

Fitted with genuine 18-ton forged axles, factory-sealed maintenance-free bearings, and dual-line WABCO ABS air braking systems for minimum stopping distance and tire wear protection.
