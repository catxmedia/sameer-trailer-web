OUR JOURNEY & MANUFACTURING HERITAGE

Founded in 1991 in Bhilwara, Rajasthan, SAMEER TRAILER MOTOR BODY PVT. LTD. has grown into one of North India's most trusted heavy commercial vehicle bodybuilders. Over 35 years of hands-on structural engineering have enabled us to pioneer low-tare-weight trailers that withstand India's toughest mining and highway operating conditions.

Operating out of twin specialized manufacturing hubs in Bhilwara, our facilities feature automated CNC plate bending, SAW beam welding lines, Sa 2.5 white-metal shot-blasting chambers, and dedicated axle alignment pits.
