BROCHURE CORPORATE OVERVIEW

OUR JOURNEY SINCE 1991

Established in 1991 in the industrial hub of Bhilwara, Rajasthan, SAMEER TRAILER MOTOR BODY PVT. LTD. has emerged as an undisputed pioneer in heavy commercial vehicle body engineering. Over 35 years of relentless innovation, we have transformed traditional heavy trailer fabrication into an automated, high-precision engineering science.

Our core philosophy centers on low tare weight optimization. By transitioning to certified SAIL & Jindal HSFQ-450 High-Tensile Steel, Sameer Trailers allow transporters to carry up to +1.0 Ton of legal extra freight payload per trip, yielding up to ₹2,50,000 in additional annual revenue per vehicle.

🎯

OUR CORPORATE MISSION

To design, manufacture, and deliver the safest, most durable, and fuel-efficient commercial trailers in India. We aim to maximize transporter profitability by integrating certified high-tensile steel, Sa 2.5 shot-blasting rust protection, and factory-warranted forged axle systems.

👁️

OUR VISION FOR 2030

To expand our twin Bhilwara manufacturing hubs into India's most advanced automated trailer production complex, setting national benchmarks for zero-crack chassis geometry, 500-hour salt-spray paint durability, and 100% on-time delivery across Indian logistics routes.
